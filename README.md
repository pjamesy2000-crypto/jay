Create an original, joyful Yoruba children's gospel song titled **"Jésù Fẹ́ràn Àwọn Ọmọdé" (Jesus Loves the Children)**.

**Language:** Sing entirely in clear, natural Yoruba. Do not sing in English.

**Theme:** Jesus loves children, protects them, guides them and welcomes them. Teach children to love God, obey their parents, share with others and pray every day.

**Music style:** Cheerful African children's gospel, lively and playful, with a catchy melody that children can easily remember and sing along to.

**Vocals:** Warm, sweet, youthful lead singing with a joyful children's choir responding in the chorus. Clear Yoruba pronunciation and natural singing.

**Instruments:** Piano, acoustic guitar, gentle African percussion, handclaps, drums, warm bass and joyful melodic accents.

**Tempo:** Upbeat, approximately 110 BPM.

**Structure:**
- Short musical introduction
- Verse 1
- Catchy repeated chorus
- Verse 2
- Chorus
- Short joyful bridge
- Final chorus with children's choir
- Gentle ending

**Length:** Approximately 3–4 minutes.

Make it sound like a professionally produced, joyful African children's worship song. Use original lyrics and an unforgettable chorus.
